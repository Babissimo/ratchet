Privacy and terms

Last updated 23 November 2026

Privacy

Ratchet has no account, no analytics and no tracking. Your time entries go between your Mac and FreeAgent, and nowhere else.

On your Mac

  • Your FreeAgent sign-in tokens, in the macOS Keychain. Logging out of Ratchet deletes them.
  • The last task you tracked, in Ratchet's preferences, so it can offer that task again.
  • Your clients, projects, tasks and recent time entries, held in memory while Ratchet runs. macOS's standard networking may also keep a short-lived cache of FreeAgent's responses in Ratchet's Caches folder.

FreeAgent

Ratchet reads and writes your clients, projects, tasks and timeslips through FreeAgent's API, as you ask it to. That data is held by FreeAgent under FreeAgent's own privacy policy. You can revoke Ratchet's access at any time from FreeAgent's settings.

The sign-in service

FreeAgent requires an app secret that can't ship inside an open-source app, so a small service at auth.ratchet.babissimo.net adds it when Ratchet signs in and when it renews access, about once an hour. Your sign-in tokens pass through it on the way to your Mac, encrypted so that only your copy of Ratchet can open them. It does not store or log them, and it never sees your time entries.

To stop one client flooding it, the service counts requests per IP address over a one-minute window. That count is not kept beyond the window. The service runs on Cloudflare, which processes request data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect it, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. The service's source code is public.

Feedback

"Send feedback" opens a new issue on Ratchet's public GitHub repository in your browser, with your Ratchet and macOS versions filled in. Nothing is sent unless you submit it, and what you submit is public and held by GitHub under GitHub's privacy statement.

This website

This site is static and served by Cloudflare. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The home page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google for them, under Google's privacy policy.
